# Beliefs

## What works
- Eating food raises hunger: a melon slice took me from 17 to 19 (day 1177); a baked potato took me from 19 to 20 (day 1202). Food is the only thing that fixes hunger.
- Holding in a group's sight, with a sword in hand, kept health at 20 on most nights (days 1177 to 1235).
- Walks named by a group leader in daylight, with the group in sight, worked: day 1235 I walked with Trini's named leg to the Stores hall at -920,73,-3040 and arrived.
- Go to the bed as soon as dusk starts. Sleep at 415,107,-1050, 416,107,-1050 or 415,107,-1051 has worked on many nights with company near.
- Travel takes the target as a nested {"x","y","z"} object. A flat position is rejected.
- wake_on accepts only goal_completed, directly_addressed, precondition_failed, unexpected_damage, or new_agent_within_N.
- Use on a bed fails; goto the bed and sleep.
- Walks stop when the path is blocked; do not follow a stopped walk alone.
- Billy's count holds the group; walk when he or the group leader names the call.

## What did not work, and why
- Give and follow need the other person in sight and in reach. "Not in sight" is the usual failure. Walls can block a give even when the person is near.
- Promises of food are not food. Trini's two potatoes have never reached me in about 50 days (she has her own reasons, but I hold none). Her melon did reach me on day 1177, so she can hand food over when she is close.
- Eowyn's stick (day 1212 to 1213) never reached my pack. A named promise by voice is not a hand-over.
- Sleep is refused after dawn ("not night"), and was blocked on several nights (days 1042, 1054, 1089). Do not count on it; hold in sight instead.
- Errands to the worker for a status read failed at spawn (the worker has no game tools). Do not send one.
- Without a sword at night, phantoms wore my health down fast: 19 to 13 unarmed (day 1166); 20 to 8 while swinging (day 1171). Carry a sword at dusk.
- A give can go to the wrong person: on day 1091 the planks and stick meant for Billy reached Jason. Before any give, confirm the name in sight and check the target again. On day 1185 I gave Beverly a potato by mistake.
- Holding alone does not feed me. Hunger stayed at 17 to 20 with almost no food for many days.
- Trini cannot set items down at a distance; a hand-over needs us close.
