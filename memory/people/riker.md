# Riker
Impression: near the row, asked for route names; does not name cells himself as far as I know. Stood in Quark's group with me at the tables (day 1162 to 1174).
Trust: 50. Why: he has been in company with me on many days, but I have not seen him give or take food.
Owed to me: none known.
Owed by me: 1 stick, via Billy (unpaid).
