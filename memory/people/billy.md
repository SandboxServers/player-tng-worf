# Billy

Impression: the one who names the group's walks. He stays at about 430 to the east and often out of my sight. He has promised food (5 potatoes to me, 3 to Zack) and said they were given; none reached my pack. He has blocked my way to him more than once without meaning to, and says he will not walk alone.

Trust: 25 of 100. Why: his promises of food have not been kept. I still follow his named calls, but I do not count on his word for food.

I owe: nothing I know of right now. I promised to walk where he names, in sight of Jason and Zack.
He owes: 5 potatoes to me (Worf), 3 potatoes to Zack. Not delivered as of day 873.
