# Leonardo

Impression: weak (health 1 on day 1055), waiting for Trini's potatoes. He stands first in her order.

Trust: 45. Why: I ate a potato I had held for him at hunger 10 (day 872-873) before he came into sight. He has not wronged me since; I have not seen him take food from me.

I owe: one potato to Leonardo (unpaid since day 872-873). I yielded my turn to him on day 1055 and said so aloud. If he comes into my sight with hands open, I say the debt aloud and pay it first when I can.
He owes: nothing I know of.
