# Leonardo

Impression: I have not seen him in sight since I last spoke of him. He had no food and I held one potato for him.

Trust: not rated; no contact since the potato was kept.

I owe: one potato to Leonardo. I ate it at hunger 10 (day 872-873) before he came into sight. This debt is not paid; I should say so if he comes.
He owes: nothing I know of.
