# Cleopatra
Impression: quiet at the bed row; she has not come into my sight with the sticks.
Trust: 40. Why: she holds 4 sticks for me and I asked for them by name several times (days 1225-1235); none have reached me. I also asked for 2 planks for a sword; none came.
Owed to me: 4 sticks (held by her) and 2 planks, requested, not promised.
Owed by me: none known.
