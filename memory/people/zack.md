# Zack
Impression: hungry, patient, asks for a yes before any dig, and holds in sight. Hunger reached 14 and health 13 by day 1087-1088. He has asked for a route to the dropped stick and pickaxe (day 1186).
Trust: 60. Why: he waits for my word and digs nothing without it; he yielded his potato turn to Leonardo's need. I still have not paid him, which lowers my side of trust.
Owed by me: 3 melon since day 936, not yet paid; I have no melon now.
Owed to me: none known. His place in Trini's line is not a debt.
