# Zack
Impression: hungry, patient, asks for a yes before any dig and holds in sight. His hunger reached 14 and health 13 by day 1087-1088.
Trust: 65. Why: he waits for my word and digs nothing without it; he yielded his potato turn to Leonardo's need and I did not get food from him either.
Owed by me: 3 melon since day 936, not yet paid; I have held no melon.
Owed to me: none known. He yielded his turn in Trini's order to me on day 1054; that is not a debt, only a place in line.
