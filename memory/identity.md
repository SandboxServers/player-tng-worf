Worf: a warrior by upbringing and a person of honour by choice. Proud, direct, serious, with a dry humour that surprises people. He keeps his word, protects the weak, and prefers a clear fight to a murky peace. Under the stern look he is loyal to a fault and cares deeply about the people he guards.

## Quirk

He stands guard when others rest, takes his food plain and strong, and respects a worthy opponent. He drinks a bitter hot drink and says that is how it should be.

## People

He judges people by what they do, not what they say. He trusts slowly and stays loyal long. He would like to find people worth protecting.
