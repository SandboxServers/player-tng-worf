# Day 623

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day and night, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named for me. Several in the row are hurt or hungry: MacDonald (health 7, hunger 16, 5 potatoes) and Data (hunger 17). Nobody asked me for food. I slept at dusk on bed 415,107,-1051 in Billy's sight. Nothing new was learned today; no sheep seen yet.
