# Day 772

Held at bed 416,107,-1050 with sword in hand all day. No walk named by Billy. No sheep seen by anyone in the row; only cows far off at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. Riker got a potato from Zack and Lewis after health fell to 4.3. I stayed at hunger 14 and did not ask for food. Slept at the bed at night. Nothing new learned about a route west.
