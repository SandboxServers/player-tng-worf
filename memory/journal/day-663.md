# Day 663

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1050 with sword in hand and hunger 18, no food. Spent the day holding in Billy's sight at the row. Said I would hold and await his word on Zack's potato and on any walk. Billy named escorts at the chest and gave Beverly a wooden sword; no walk was named for me. Wesley is waiting for Clark's spare sword in daylight. Nobody has seen sheep. Night came, and I slept in Billy's sight at bed 416,107,-1050.
