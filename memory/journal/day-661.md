# Day 661

Held at my bed 416,107,-1050 in Billy's sight with my sword in hand all day. No walk was named for me. Tried to sleep at morning; refused because it was not night. Hunger fell from 18 and I did not eat, since it had not reached 10. Heard the row: Tommy, MacDonald and Data hungry and waiting for Billy's word; sheep still unseen (Leonardo asked again). At dusk slept at my bed with the row.
