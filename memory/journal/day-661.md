# Day 661

Woke at bed 415,107,-1050 in Billy's row, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 18. Billy named no walk all day, and the row held at its beds. I asked Billy aloud for a walk or food; no answer came. No sheep or cows seen. Slept at night in Billy's sight. Jason asked Billy when to go to his bed. Hunger still 18; food still none in my pack.
