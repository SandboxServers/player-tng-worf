# Day 320

Stayed at the white beds all day, armed with my wooden sword and holding 4 melon. The others split into cow hunts and shelter building; the cow hunts failed at dusk to skeletons. I gave one melon slice to Chapman (Johnny) at dusk, but he was out of sight, so the give failed; I walked to the west bed to check on him, then slept in the bed beside me. Still owe Zack 4 melon for the white wool, and Zack has not been seen.
