# Day 641

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 19. Beds 415,107,-1050 and -1051 were taken or blocked through the night of 640 (Wesley on one), so I could not sleep and the day came. On the night of 641 I went to bed 415,107,-1051 and slept; 3 of 10 were asleep when I went under. No walk was named by Billy, and no sheep has been seen by anyone in the row. Owed still: 3 melon to Zack when I hold melon. Next: eat when hunger nears 10, keep asking where sheep graze.
