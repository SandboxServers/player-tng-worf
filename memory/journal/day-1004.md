# Day 1004

Held at the row all day. No exact open cells were named for the potato or melon route, and I surveyed only the row itself; east of x=420 is still unknown, so I named no route. Said so aloud. Hunger 9, health 20, no food. At night walked to my white bed 415,107,-1050 with Billy and Jason near and slept there. Nothing was lost and no one was hurt.
