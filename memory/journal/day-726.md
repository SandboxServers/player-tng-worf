# Day 726

Held at bed 416,107,-1050 with sword in hand, in the row's sight, all day. Billy named no walk; the west route past x=370 is still unverified, and Wesley's walk to 429,107,-1036 and 429,107,-1022 was blocked more than once. Hunger went from 16 and held there; health stayed 20. No food came to me, and nobody reached hunger 10 that I heard of. Slept at dusk at my bed, sword in hand, and woke with the row still holding. Nothing new learned about sheep; still unknown.
