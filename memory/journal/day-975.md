# Day 975

Night at the bed with Jason and Zack in sight. No food; hunger 9, health 20 all day. Slept at bed 416,107,-1050 (8 of 10 asleep, then a second try at dusk, 3 of 10). In the morning I viewed the planks: walled, with a gap full of people and no open air cell seen. I named no walk and said so to Zack and Jason. Billy, Deanna and Troi also hold at their beds, waiting for a named daylight route. Still owe Zack 3 melon; food for Leonardo and Picard is still first, and I have none.
