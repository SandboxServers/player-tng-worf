# Day 429

Walked to the 387 row in daylight and joined Billy's group of twelve for the count. Holding for the call to walk northeast to the wheat field for water and sheep, but no call came. Zack received the wooden sword from Clark. I still hold no food; Jason has bread and Chapman has rotten flesh for anyone at hunger 6 or below. At dusk I went to the white bed at 387,107,-1036 in Billy's sight and slept. Health 20, hunger 20.
