# Day 1003

Woke at my white bed in the row with Billy, Jason and Zack near. Health 20, hunger 9, no food. Many in the row are hungry and holding; Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Zack first. I surveyed the cells between my bed and Trini's, but east of x=420 is still unknown, so I named no route and did not walk. Night came; I slept at my bed with company near.
