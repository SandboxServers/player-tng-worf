# Day 644

Dawn came while I tried to sleep at bed 415,107,-1051; the first sleep was blocked and the second was refused as not night. I held at the bed in Billy's sight with my sword in hand, health 20, hunger 19. At dusk I slept on bed 415,107,-1051 and six of ten were asleep; I woke at morning with health 20 and hunger 19. No walk was named. Deanna and Tommy are still hungry, MacDonald and Clark hold the potatoes, and no one has seen sheep. I carry no food.
