# Day 976

Walled still by the planks at the row. No open air seen past them in view, so no walk was named. No food held all day; hunger 9, health 20. Held by the bed 416 with Jason and Zack in reach through the night and the morning. Heard that Leonardo is weak and hungry, Laforge, Riker, Troi and Billy all hold at their beds waiting for a daylight route from me. I have none to give yet. Slept at dusk.
