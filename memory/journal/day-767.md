# Day 767

Held at the row all day at bed 416,107,-1050 with sword in hand, health 20, hunger 14. Billy named no walk. Sleep was refused in daylight, so I waited. Zack offered a potato at hunger 14; I declined until hunger 10, as Billy set. Jason tried the second bed at 415,107,-1051 and found it not usable. John (Chapman) is hungry at health 9 and asked for potatoes; Lewis and Zack hold them. Night came at the end of day 768 and I stay at the row. Nothing new learned about sheep.
