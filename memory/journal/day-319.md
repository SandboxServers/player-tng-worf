# Day 319

Night at the white beds. My give of two melon to Picard failed because he was out of sight, and Picard was fed later from Chapman and Macdonald. Lewis was still weak and out of sight. Data was boxed in by doors with no food; he came out and asked for melon, and I offered but he was not in my sight. Chapman's spare wooden sword was given to me by mistake, and I passed it to Macdonald for the cow hunt at 370,93,-1124, which has a skeleton near it. I kept my own sword and 4 melon, still owing Zack 4 melon for the white wool. I slept at the beds.
