# Day 652

Woke at bed 415,107,-1050 in Billy's sight, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 18. No walk was named all day, and no food was needed yet. I held at the bed, speaking little, while the row counted itself morning and dusk. At night I went to the bed at 415,107,-1050 and rested in sight of the group. Nothing was lost. Still no sheep or cows seen by me.
