Day 974. Still walled by the planks of the row; the gap is full of people and no open air cell has been seen in several views. No daylight walk was named, so none was taken. Health held at 20, hunger 9, no food all day. Trini said she holds potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack, and I asked her to name where she stands; no answer yet. Sleep was refused at the bed on the first dusk (way blocked) and accepted on the second night. Holding by the beds with Jason and Zack in sight was the whole of the day.
