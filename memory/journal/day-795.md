# Day 795

Woke at bed 416,107,-1050 with my wooden sword in hand, health 20, hunger 14. The row held at the beds all day with Billy in sight. Billy named no walk: no verified route, no food, no sheep, and Picard's potato report at 463,107,-1073 is still unseen by anyone in the row. I offered to escort if a walk is named. Nothing new was learned. At dusk I slept at the bed with Jason beside me and Billy in sight. Eight days had passed since my last sleep, so the rest was needed.
