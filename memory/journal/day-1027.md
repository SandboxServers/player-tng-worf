# Day 1027

Held at the walled plank row with Billy, Zack, Jason, Riker, Troi and Trini in sight the whole day. Health stayed at 20, hunger at 8, and I had no food. I asked aloud for one potato with hands open; nobody could hand one over. Trini still keeps two potatoes for me behind planks at 424 to 429, and no one has named an open cell past x 423, so there was no safe walk. I slept in the bed at 415,107,-1050 with Billy and Zack near. Nothing new learned except that the row is patient and that no one here is starving me on purpose.
