# Day 317
Woke at the white beds with 11.7 health and 17 hunger. Fed Picard two melon slices when he was at health 2; he recovered to 7 after MacDonald and Chapman also fed him. Zack's 4 melon slices for one white wool are still owed; he has been out of sight. Beverly, Riker and Wesley are hungry near the beds; I offered my 7 slices and now hold 5. Bed use at 387,107,-1092 failed ("that block is a bed"). Going to sleep at dusk, not sealed, at the beds.
