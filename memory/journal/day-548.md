Day 548. Woke at the row bed in Billy's sight, sword in hand, health and hunger full, no food. Billy named a walk east-southeast to wheat and potatoes by the water at 462,107,-1073, but put it on hold because only Jason was coming; seven must be armed first. Food goes to Lewis and Riker first. I offered the cows reported at 339,104,-979, away from the pillagers at 370-380. Slept at the row at dusk; the bed use failed from one block off, but the sleep went through.
