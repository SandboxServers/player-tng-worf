# Day 470

Dusk at the row beside bed 401,107,-1023. Still no sword in my hand; the others also struggle to get wooden swords into hand, so Billy has not called a walk (count two of four armed). I went to bed at the row in Billy's sight. A phantom came from the west at dusk; Jason took a bed, and I went into mine. Sleep failed while it was still dusk and worked once night came. Nothing was eaten; I still hold no food, hunger 20, health 20.
