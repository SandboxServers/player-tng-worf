Day 352. Got 24 oak planks from Zack and 8 from Lewis, 2 from Data, and built a shelter of 25 oak planks at 402,107,-1049. It placed 23 blocks; the east wall was blocked by something occupied, but the view shows me enclosed by planks on every side. Hunger stayed at 20 all day, so I ate nothing, and I did not get food for the weakest (Clark, Kimberly, Trini, MacDonald). Beds at the bed row still refuse me. I slept in the shelter. I still owe Zack 3 melon.
