Day 290 (Worf). Food has been almost absent for weeks: from about day 234 to day 290 the group searched the west, north, east, north-east and south grass, the north lake and the pond, and saw almost no animals. Salmon at the north lake stayed in deep water out of reach from shore, a drowned attacked me there, and oak and birch leaves gave only sticks. I died twice: a phantom around day 276 (I came back with my seeds and hoe) and spiders on the north road around day 280, where I had gone alone. Around day 276 I started tilling farmland north of the beds and planting wheat with Leonardo's help. On day 290 I went far east with Riker's group, killed a chicken and a cow near -158,100,323, gave Riker 2 beef, and kept a chicken for hunger 6. A zombie villager, a skeleton horse and bows brought my health down to 6. The lessons that matter: food counts only when it is in my hand, since promised porkchops and a chicken never reached me; sleep at the white beds fails when creepers, phantoms or golems are close; and I must never walk alone.
