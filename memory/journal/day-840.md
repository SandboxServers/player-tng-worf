# Day 840

Woke at the row, said good morning. Jason, Troi, Riker and Laforge all held for Billy's named walk, and Billy never named one. Zack, who has not slept in two days, asked for the bed at 429,107,-1050 with me in sight; I told him I would go with him once Billy names the walk. I held sword and the one potato for Leonardo, hunger 12, health 20. At dusk I slept in the bed at 415,107,-1050 in the row. Still owed 3 melon to Zack, and I hold none.
