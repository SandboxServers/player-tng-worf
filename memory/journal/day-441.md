# Day 441

Woke at night beside the white beds at 388,107,-1023, alone. At dawn I walked to the 388,107,-1037 row when Billy called, and held there in his sight with about ten others. No food was found; hunger stayed at 20 but food is scarce across the group. At dusk my try to use a bed failed ("that block is a bed"), so I went to a bed near 401,107,-1035 and slept there, alone in view of no one. Billy has asked that we walk only in groups of four or more. I still have no named cell. Owed: 3 melon to Zack when I hold melon.
