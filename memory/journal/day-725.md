# Day 725

Held at bed 416,107,-1050 with sword in hand all day. Billy named no walk. Wesley's walks to 429,107,-1036 and 429,107,-1022 were blocked and he held in the row. Hunger stayed at 16, so I did not ask for potatoes. Slept at dusk in the row's sight; 5 of 10 were asleep. Nothing new learned about sheep or cows. Trini and Laforge joined the row's talk. Nobody has verified a route past x=370.
