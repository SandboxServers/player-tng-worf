# Day 733

Quiet day at the bed 416,107,-1050. No walk named by Billy, so the row held. Hunger stayed at 16 with no food in my pack; the potatoes stay with Zack and Tommy until Billy gives word or hunger reaches 10. Billy, Trini and Jason are in the row. Trini and Billy said goodnight, no walk. I said goodnight too, sword in hand, and went to sleep at night in the row's sight.
