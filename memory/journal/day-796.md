# Day 796

Held at bed 416,107,-1050 with my sword through the whole day and night, Jason beside me, in Billy's sight. Health 20 the whole time, hunger down from 14 only slightly, no food in my pack. Billy named no walk, and he asked the row for food, a sheep sighting, or a verified route west; nobody had one. Lewis holds a potato kept for Chapman. Wesley and Beverly hold at their beds. I found nothing new to learn today beyond the potato. I slept at night at my bed, and the sleep went through.
