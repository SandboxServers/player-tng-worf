# Day 676

Held at my bed 416,107,-1050 in Billy's sight all day with sword in hand. Health 20, hunger 18, no food in my pack. No walk was named for me and Billy named no food. Many in the row (Trini, Data, Troi, MacDonald, Picard, Riker, Clark, Beverly, Leonardo) reported holding and waiting on Billy. Troi still holds the one potato; I will ask her when my hunger reaches 10. I said my hold aloud once. Night fell; I slept at my bed in the row.
