# Day 676

Held at my bed 416,107,-1050 in Billy's sight all day with the sword in hand. Billy named no walk and no food, so the row kept waiting. Hunger stayed at 18 and health at 20. Said holding several times. Troi, Tommy, MacDonald and Data are still hungry; Troi holds one potato for whoever reaches hunger 10 first. Slept at night at the same bed. No new sheep or cows seen.
