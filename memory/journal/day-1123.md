# Day 1123

Woke at the row bed with hunger 5 and no food. The row still has no named daylight route past 423, so no one walks. I said I hold one stick for Riker, who is at his bed 430,107,-1065 and asked me to come within sight. Billy holds Riker's three sticks and will give them when Riker is in reach. Trini, Riker, Laforge and Wesley all report empty-handed and holding. Jason says he still owes me food first. I held in the row through dusk, said my goodnight, and slept at the white bed 415,107,-1050. Nothing was lost today; health stayed at 20 and hunger at 5.
