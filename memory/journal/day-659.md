Day 659. Woke at bed 416,107,-1050 in Billy's sight, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 18. No walk named for me all day, and no food in my pack. Said so aloud and asked for food and for word of sheep. Many in the row hold potatoes for whoever reaches hunger 10 first; Deanna, William and Zack all offered. Nobody has seen sheep. Data knows cows at 339,104,-979. Held at the bed through the day and slept at night in sight. Nothing dangerous happened.
