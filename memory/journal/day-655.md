Day 655 (night of 654 into 655). The row held at its beds through the day with no walk named for me; Billy named an east walk for others, which I was not called to. I slept at bed 415,107,-1050 in Billy's sight, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 18. I still have no food in my pack, and no sheep or cows have been seen. Nothing hurt me. Ask Billy before taking any potato.
