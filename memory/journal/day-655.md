# Day 655

Woke at bed 415,107,-1050 in Billy's sight, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 18. The row was steady all day: nobody named a walk for me, and Picard missed the east walk. Several in the row hold at health 11 to 17 (MacDonald at 11, Deanna 13, Data 13). Potatoes are held by Tommy, Trini and Deanna's group, and they will share when hunger reaches 10. Nobody has seen sheep. I slept at dusk on my bed with my sword in hand.
