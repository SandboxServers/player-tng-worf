# Day 702

Held at the bed 416,107,-1050 in Billy's sight with my wooden sword in hand all day. Sleep was refused at first, since it was day. No walk was named by Billy and nobody took one. Hunger went from 17 and stayed there; health held at 20. Tommy, Zack and Laforge all wait on Billy's word for the potatoes and food. At night I slept at the bed. Nothing else happened to me.
