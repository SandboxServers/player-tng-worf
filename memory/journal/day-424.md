# Day 424

Woke at the 401 bed row with Billy's group and counted. I held no food all day and could give none. Food is scarce: Laforge, Jason and Lewis passed bread and melon to the weakest. Kimberly's pack was gone when we reached it. Clark's and Data's oak logs were handed over by Chapman. At dusk I slept by a white bed, in the group's sight, but the sleep moved me to another row near 429,-1049, where Clark stood beside me. Hunger 20, health 20 all day.
