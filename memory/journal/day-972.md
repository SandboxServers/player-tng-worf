# Day 972 (latest entry in pending.md)

Days 950 to 972 mattered most in one way: I was walled into the plank row at the beds near 415,107,-1050 for weeks and never once confirmed an open cell, so no daylight route was ever named and nobody walked. I held a stone sword and stayed by the beds with Zack and Jason in reach, with Billy, Riker, Troi, Laforge, Lewis and Clark nearby. Food never came: I held none from day 933 on, hunger fell from 10 to 9 by day 967, health stayed at 20, but Leonardo (health 1 at day 963) and Picard (health 2.3 at day 938) were the urgent needs and I could not meet them. Sleep at the bed worked on some nights and was blocked on others; holding in company when it failed worked better than walking in the dark. Promised food (potatoes from Beverly, Leonardo and Billy) never reached my pack. I still owe Zack 3 melon, and I have not yet seen a melon.
