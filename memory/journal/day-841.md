# Day 841

Held at the row by the white beds all night and morning with Jason, Zack, Troi, Riker and Laforge. Billy named no walk. Greeted the row at morning. Health stayed at 20, hunger 12, and I kept my sword and the one potato for Leonardo. Nothing attacked us. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at bed 415,107,-1050. Still owed 3 melon to Zack; I hold none.
