# Day 719

Held at the bed 416,107,-1050 with my sword in hand all day, Jason beside me. Billy named no walk. Health 20, hunger 16, no food. The row kept saying good morning and holding. Zack offered a potato to Troi; not for me to take. At dusk I slept at the bed and the night passed. Nothing new was learned except that the row still waits on Billy.
