# Day 977

Woke at my bed in the walled plank row, health 20, hunger 9, no food. The row waited all day for a named daylight walk. I kept saying I would name the north-west route once it was full light, but the light stayed dim, then clear, then turned to dusk without ever being full day. I did not walk in the dusk. I told the row I was holding and went to bed at my bed with Jason and Zack in reach. Still no food for Leonardo or Picard, and the potatoes owed to me have not come.
