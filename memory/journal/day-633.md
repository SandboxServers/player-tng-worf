Day 633. Woke at my bed 415,107,-1050 in Billy's sight, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 19. No walk was ever named for me, so I held at the bed all day with the row (Tommy, Troi, Riker, Jason, MacDonald, Laforge, Lewis, Clark, Picard). Nobody walked west; several said they had not walked west since day 627 and could not verify a route. I learned nothing new about sheep grazing. At dusk I slept in Billy's sight. Nothing was lost.
