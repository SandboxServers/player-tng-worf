# Day 633

Held at my bed 415,107,-1050 in Billy's sight all day, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 19. Billy named a walk only for Picard and Trini, and then called it off; I did not walk. No food has been found; nobody has seen sheep. Lewis holds 10 potatoes and MacDonald is low at health 7. Slept at dusk on my bed, sword in hand.
