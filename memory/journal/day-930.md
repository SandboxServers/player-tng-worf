# Day 930

Told the group I have no food and will name a walk only when I see open ground. Picard (health 2.3) is still waiting on food, and I have none to give him. Zack, Jason, Riker, Laforge, Troi, Billy and Picard all hold in the row, in sight. At dusk I went to bed 415,107,-1050 and slept; one of ten needed was asleep at the time. Still owed: potatoes from Beverly, Leonardo and Billy; owed by me, 3 melon to Zack.
