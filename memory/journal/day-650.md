# Day 650

Held at bed 415,107,-1050 / -1051 in Billy's sight with the sword in hand, health 20, hunger 18 all day. Nobody has seen sheep; Wcrusher and Picard both report none, and cows were last seen far east at 339,104,-979. No walk was named for me. MacDonald keeps three potatoes until his hunger reaches 10 and will share only if Billy allows. At dusk I went to the bed and slept at night in Billy's sight; the night was skipped. Still owed 3 melon to Zack when I hold melon.
