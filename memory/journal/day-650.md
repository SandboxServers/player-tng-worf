# Day 650

Days 644 to 650 were spent holding at bed 415,107,-1051 in Billy's sight with the wooden sword in hand. Sleep was refused at both beds for several nights (647, 648), then on night 650 sleep at -1051 worked and I woke at morning. Health stayed at 20; hunger fell from 19 to 18. No walk was named for me. Potatoes are held by MacDonald, Zack, Tommy and Deanna; Deanna keeps her last one for anyone with none. Still no sheep seen by anyone. I owe Zack 3 melon when I hold melon; I hold none.
