# Day 280 (Worf)

Woke at the white beds with the group, full at 20, no food. The saplings and logs I had picked up were meant for Lewis; my give went to Riker by mistake, so I asked Riker to pass them on. Food stayed scarce all day: the wheat near -266,66,322 gave seeds, not food, and LaForge got the chicken first, then Trini. Picard came asking at hunger 4; I had nothing to give. Golems pressed from the south and east through the afternoon. At dusk I slept at a white bed with the group.
