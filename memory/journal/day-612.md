# Day 612
Held at my bed 415,107,-1051 in Billy's sight all day with my sword in hand. No walk was named for me; Billy kept the row on hold until a route was verified. Wesley's bed refused sleep again and he was told to try his own bed at dusk. Jason was allowed back to his bed at 416,107,-1050. At night I slept on my own bed and the night was skipped. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Nothing new learned except that the row waits on Billy's named call.
