# Day 443
Night at Billy's row, in sight of the group. Still no food; hunger 20, health 20. Tried to sleep in a bed beside the row: the bed use was refused ("that block is a bed") and sleep twice took too long, then the goto to my named cell 413,107,-1041 let me sleep at last. I stayed close to others all night and walked nowhere alone. Lesson: walking to a bed takes me into the dark only if I walk alone; sleep came when I stood beside the bed and called sleep. Next: at first light, walk to Billy's row with the group, ask for food, and hold for Billy's call.
