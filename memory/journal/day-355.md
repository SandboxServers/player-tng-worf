# Day 355

The cow walk stalled all morning: Billy waited for the whole group to be in sight, and nobody was hungry enough to need it yet. I held at the bed row with the group, no food, health 20, hunger 20. At dusk I went to the bed at 401,107,-1050 and slept there in sight of the group. MacDonald offered a white wool for Data; I said yes and asked him to bring it to me at the bed. Clark is still looking for water to the west. Kimberly, Trini, Clark and MacDonald are the lowest on food, and they hold the melon and meat for each other. I have not yet taught anyone the shelter, and I still owe Zack 3 melon.
