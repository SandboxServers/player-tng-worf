# Day 698

Held in the row at the beds with my sword in hand all day, no walk named by Billy. My rest intent at the row finished at once without sleep. Hunger stayed at 17, health 20. At dusk I walked to my bed beside 416,107,-1050 and slept there in Billy's sight; the sleep worked. Wesley and Picard report the beds refusing and then working, so bed trouble comes and goes. Nobody has seen sheep. Still no food beyond Tommy's potatoes, which I ask for at hunger 10.
