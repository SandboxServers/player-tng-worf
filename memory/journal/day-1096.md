# Day 1096 (journal from days 1042 to 1096)

Most of these days were spent holding at the walled plank row, in company with Billy, Jason, Zack, Wesley and Riker, and never walking past cell 423 at z -1051. No open cell east was ever confirmed by two readers; planks and solid blocks kept blocking the view, and I named no route I had not seen. Trini has held two potatoes at 430,107,-1051 for many days, ordered Leonardo (health 1), then Zack, then me. Zack yielded to me once, and I yielded to Leonardo and Zack; the potatoes never reached me. Hunger has fallen from 7 to 5 and health has stayed at 20 by holding in sight, sleeping near company, and keeping my stone sword. The one walk toward Trini (day 1050) stopped at 418 when the way was blocked. Two gifts went astray: the 2 planks and stick I meant for Billy reached Jason by mistake (day 1091), and I still owe 3 melon to Zack. The lesson that matters most: a promise of food is not food, and an unseen route is not a route.
