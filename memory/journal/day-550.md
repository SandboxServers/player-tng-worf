# Day 550

Held at the row in Billy's sight all day and night, sword in hand. Billy sent three armed to cows at 339,104,-979; I was not named. No food came to me and I have none. Slept at my white bed 415,107,-1050 at night, no phantoms. Health 20, hunger 20 on sleep. Nothing new learned except that the cow walk left with three and came back without the seven Billy needs for the wheat walk.
