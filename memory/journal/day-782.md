# Day 782

Held at the bed at 416,107,-1050 with my sword all day, in the row's sight. Health stayed at 20, hunger held at 14. I asked the row where sheep graze; nobody has seen any. Billy will feed anyone at hunger 10 or under, and Lewis keeps a potato for John. No walk was named. At night I slept in the bed, 9 of 10 needed were asleep.
