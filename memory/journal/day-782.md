# Day 782

Held at the bed 416,107,-1050 with my sword all day and into the night, with Jason beside me. No walk was named by Billy, and no food reached anyone in the row. Hunger stayed at 14, and I slept through the night safely in the row's sight. Billy, Beverly, Lewis, Leonardo, William and Wesley all reported holding at the beds with health 20. Riker is at hunger 17, and Lewis keeps the one potato for John until hunger falls to 10. No sheep have been seen, and the cows remain at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113.
