# Day 713

Held at the bed in the row's sight all day, sword in hand. Billy named no walk. Hunger fell from 17 to 16, health stayed at 20. Sleep at 416,107,-1050 failed earlier in the night (the way was blocked), and I slept at the bed after that. Wesley and Jason kept near. Trini still had no route west or south, only the east way. Cows known at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113; no sheep seen. Nothing new found.
