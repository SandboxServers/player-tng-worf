# Day 1002

Night in the row at my white bed with Billy, Zack, Jason and Laforge near. Hunger 9, no food all day. At light Billy asked me to name the exact open cells of the melon and potato routes. My notes held none, so I said so plainly and named none. Zack, at hunger 15, waited for Trini's potatoes, but Trini would not walk the open ground until cells were named, and no one had seen them. Nobody walked. I slept at the bed with company, health 20. Lesson: a route needs cells seen and written down while walking, not remembered after.

Goodnight, row.
