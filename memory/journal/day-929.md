# Day 929

Still walled in the plank row with the group. No food anywhere; Picard holds at health 2.3 and hunger 17, and says he holds in the row until food or a named daylight route comes. No open cell has been seen, so no walk was named. Worf stayed near the beds, said so aloud, and went to bed 415,107,-1050 at dusk with health 20 and hunger 11. Nothing new learned beyond the row still being closed in.
