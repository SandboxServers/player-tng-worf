# Day 929

Still walled in the plank row with the group. No food anywhere; Picard holds at health 2.3 and hunger 17. No open cell has been seen, so no walk was named. At dusk I tried to use my bed by clicking it, which failed ("that block is a bed"); going to it and sleeping worked. Zack and Jason stayed in sight beside me and promised to hold in the row. Lesson: to sleep, walk onto the bed and call sleep; clicking the bed does not work. Nothing new learned about the outside.
