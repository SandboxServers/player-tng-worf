Day 1132. Held at the row bed all day with Zack, Jason, Wcrusher and Billy near. Health stayed at 20, hunger fell to 4 and stayed there; no food came to me and no cell past 423 was named by two readers, so nobody walked. Sleep was refused twice when it was not night, then taken at the bed at night. Gave the stick to Riker earlier, but it went to Billy by mistake; Billy now holds the sticks for Riker. Owed still: melon to Zack, potato to Leonardo, planks and stick to Billy, stick to Riker.
