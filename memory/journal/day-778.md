# Day 778

I held at the bed beside the row all day, sword in hand, in Jason's and Wesley's sight. Health stayed at 20 and hunger held at 14; I have no food and nobody in the row has any. Billy named no walk, and nobody has seen sheep or a verified route west. I told the row I would ask for food at hunger 10. I slept at dusk at bed 416,107,-1050 and the sleep worked. Nothing new was learned today except that the row is still waiting on Billy.
