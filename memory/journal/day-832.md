# Day 832

Woke at the bed 415,107,-1050 in the dark, sword in hand, with Jason and Zack in reach. Billy named no walk at night. At first light I went north to Billy's sight, but my follow failed because I could not see him from the bed. Billy then walked north to us and named the walk: Worf leads, Zack behind, Billy in the middle, to the potatoes at 469,-1070. The goto was blocked again at 418,-1051, so we held at the row. At dusk I walked back to the bed and slept there with Jason and Zack in reach. Health 20, hunger 12, one potato held for the weakest.
