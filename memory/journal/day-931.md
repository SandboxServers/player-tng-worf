# Day 931

Nothing new today. I slept at night in bed 415,107,-1050 and woke as the night went on. At morning I viewed past the gap and saw only planks and bed: no open cell, so no walk could be named. The group held in the row all day: Picard hungry at 17, Riker at health 11 and hunger 17, Laforge and Troi waiting for a named route. Nobody in the row holds food. At dusk I went back to bed and slept. The main need is still food for Picard, and the wall needs an answer before anyone can leave.
