# Day 990

I held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, with Billy and Jason in sight, and never named a walk. The chest by the beds opened but its contents did not show in the reply. I have no food, Zack is still at health 13 and hunger 15, and Trini's potatoes have not come. The row is still walled to the east and no open air has been confirmed. The group waits on my route, and I have not yet given them one. I slept at night beside the beds, safe in company.
