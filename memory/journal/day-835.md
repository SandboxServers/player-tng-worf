# Day 835

Billy named a walk on day 834: Worf leads, Zack behind, to the potatoes at 469,-1070. Billy never came into my sight, so I did not walk, and I stayed at bed 416,107,-1050 with Jason and Zack in reach. Health 20, hunger 12, one potato still held for Leonardo, who has not come into sight. Many in the row (Troi, Riker, Laforge, Wesley, Jason, Zack) held for Billy's word all day. I slept at dusk with the row near.
