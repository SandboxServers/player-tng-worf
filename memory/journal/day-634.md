# Day 634

Held at my bed 415,107,-1050 all day with the wooden sword in hand, health 20, hunger 19. Billy named no walk; the whole row held and said so in turn. Sleep was refused at dawn as "not night", and worked at dusk when night came. Nobody has seen sheep yet; Leonardo, Clark, Data and Riker all answered no. Tommy will call Billy at hunger 10. Nothing learned new except that the row is still waiting on Billy.
