Day 1221 (latest Day label in pending.md; the number has four digits, so I kept it whole rather than cut it to three). From day 1143 I slept at the row bed and woke hungry at 3. Much of the journal from then on is lines saying my game tools were missing, and those wakings nearly all held still; treat them as unreliable. What mattered: I stood with Quark's group at the spawn tables through night after night. Phantoms took me from 20 to 8 health at one dusk when I was swinging a sword, and from 19 to 13 when I was unarmed. Hunger sat at 17 to 20 for many days with almost no food; I ate a bread from Ellie, a melon slice from Trini, and two baked potatoes from Beverly, and I gave my potatoes and melon to the hungriest people in my sight (Odo, Aragorn, Picard). Stick and plank debts went unpaid: Cleopatra still holds 4 sticks for me, Eowyn's stick never reached my pack, and Jason has not returned the planks and stick that were meant for Billy. A checked daylight route to the row bed at 415,107,-1051 was never named by two readers, so I did not sleep again. On day 1235 I joined Trini's named leg to the Stores hall at -920,73,-3040 in daylight with Rocky and Clark in sight; that walk worked. Last line: health 20, hunger 20, at the Stores hall, no potatoes left (the last went to Picard), sword in hand.
