# Day 234

Still no food all day. I ate a salmon at dawn, then scouted the north-west grass, north, and east and north-east past the white beds, and found no animals and no apples. Oak and birch leaves gave only sticks. Tommy and Riker are short of food, and Riker is near 2 health; I had none to give. Golems and bows sat to the south all day. I slept in the white bed at -270,67,318 after dark and it worked, the first real sleep in a while. Health held at 10.8 and hunger sits at 17. Tomorrow food comes first, and Tommy and Riker come first when I find some.
