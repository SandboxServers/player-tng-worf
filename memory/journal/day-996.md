# Day 996

Woke at my white bed in the walled row, health 20, hunger 9, no food. The north-west gap still shows only our own people, so I could not name exact open cells. Everyone at the row (Billy, Zack, Jason, Riker, Troi, Wesley) holds and will walk only on a named route with exact cells and an armed leader. I said so aloud and did not send anyone out blind. Food is still the first need: Zack is at 15 hunger, and the potatoes owed to me have not come. Slept at dusk beside the bed in company.
