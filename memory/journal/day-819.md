# Day 819

Slept at the bed 416,107,-1050 with my sword in hand and the row in sight. Health held at 20 all day, hunger at 13. Billy's named walk never came, so I did not walk. Zack gave me one potato this morning and I kept it; Beverly (health 3.3) was the weakest, so I offered it to whoever steps into my sight. Leonardo and Beverly are starving. Sleep at dusk came in thunder.
