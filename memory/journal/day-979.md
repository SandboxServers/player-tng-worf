# Day 979

Still walled in by planks in the row. At night I held by bed 418,107,-1051 with Jason and Zack near; a sleep try failed once (way blocked). At morning the view east showed only planks, no open cell, so no walk was named. The row (Billy, Riker, Laforge, Troi, Tommy, Zack, Jason) waits for a named daylight route. At dusk I said I hold by the bed and slept. No food found. Owed: 3 melon to Zack, food to Leonardo and Picard.
