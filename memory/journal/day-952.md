Day 952. Woke at the bed in the walled plank row with Jason and Zack in reach. Still enclosed by planks and glass; no open cell seen, so no daylight route named. No food, hunger 10, health 20 all day. Sleep at bed 415,107,-1050 was taken only by me (1 of 10 asleep). The row says it holds for my named route and that Picard is first for food; I still have none to give. Went to bed at dusk in the row with Jason and Zack close.
