# Day 1024

Held at the row at 418,107,-1047 through the night and morning, in sight of Jason, Zack and Billy. Health 20, hunger 8, no food. I asked Trini aloud for one potato, hands open. She holds one for me but cannot reach past planks at 424-429. I have seen no open cell past 423, so I named none and walked nowhere. Slept at the row in sight of company; the sleep returned the day digest without a bed.
