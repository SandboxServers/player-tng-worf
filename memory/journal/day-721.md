# Day 721

Held at the bed 416,107,-1050 all day with sword in hand. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not walk. Health stayed at 20 and hunger fell from 17 to 16. Zack offered a potato twice; I kept my rule and waited for hunger 10 and Billy's word. Beverly, Laforge, Zack, Jason and Picard all held the row the same way. Slept at night in Billy's sight, then the night was skipped. Nothing new was found about food, sheep or cows.
