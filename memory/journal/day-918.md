# Day 918

Held the night in the plank row at my white bed 415,107,-1051. Phantoms hit Laforge and Lewis, nobody died. Lewis is at health 1.3 and Picard at 2.3; nobody in the group holds food, and the potatoes owed to me have not come. At morning I viewed from my bed and surveyed the row: the only open cells I can confirm are the north gap at 417,107,-1053 to -1054, where Billy stands. I did not name a walk, because I have no confirmed route out past that gap. Everyone waits in sight for a named walk. I slept at dusk in my bed.
