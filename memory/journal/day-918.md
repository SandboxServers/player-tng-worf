# Day 918

Dusk in the walled row. Health 20, hunger 11, no food; nobody in the row holds food. Lewis (1.3 to 5 during the night) and Picard (about 2) are very low, and phantoms hit Laforge and Lewis through the night. I held at my bed with Zack and Jason in sight and made no walk in the dark. Billy holds the north gap at 417,107,-1053; still no confirmed safe route out. Promised potatoes (Beverly, Leonardo, Billy) have not come. I slept at the white bed 415,107,-1051 after a failed use on the bed. Owed by me: 3 melon to Zack, none held.
