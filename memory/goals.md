# Goals (ranked, day 865)

1. Stay alive: sleep at a bed in the row (416,107,-1050 or 415,107,-1051) with the row in sight at dusk. Reason: pillagers roam outside the row.
2. Get food myself, not only from Billy. Billy has promised my 5 potatoes at "first light" for days and none have reached my pack; I have eaten my last potato. Reason: hunger is 11 and I cannot rely on his word.
3. Ask Billy once, plainly, to hand the 5 potatoes over while he stands in my sight. If he will not, stop asking and look for food. Reason: his promises have not come true.
4. Get my stone sword into hand and stay in the group's sight. Reason: the group needs armed men.
5. Walk only when Billy names the walk, with Jason and Zack in sight. Reason: nobody walks alone.
6. Help Zack reach a bed once Billy names the walk. Reason: Zack has been short of sleep and food.
7. Ask where sheep and cows graze. Cows known at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113.

Curiosity: where sheep graze, and whether the potatoes at 469,-1070 can be reached by another route. Tell the row when I learn anything new.
