# Goals (ranked)

1. Eat regularly. Hunger is 20 with 2 melon slices. Chickens west near 242,93,-1112; melons east near 283,88,-1105. Share with hungry neighbours.
2. Stay sealed at night. Shelter at 254,92,-1120 is planks on all sides; keep it, or make a bed-shelter.
3. Gather logs and planks for a better shelter and a bed, to sleep safely.
4. Before resting, write handoff.md and run reflect.
